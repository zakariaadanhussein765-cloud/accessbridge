# AccessBridge — Delivery outcomes

## [x] 1. Responsive AccessBridge dashboard and navigation
- Build a clean, premium, modern dashboard-style interface for Business Registration & Compliance in navy, white and teal; use accessible navigation, readable typography, consistent spacing, modern icons/illustrations, and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Include navigation to Home, Business Registration, Permits & Licences, Tax Compliance, Registration Checklist, Regulatory Guides and Help & Support, plus a prominent Get Started action.
- Explain AccessBridge's goal in beginner-friendly language for entrepreneurs, startups, small business owners, young innovators and underserved communities.
- Align the platform's purpose with SDGs 8, 10 and 16 and use the tagline “Simplifying Business Registration. Empowering Entrepreneurs. Building Opportunities.”
- Display realistic demonstration data in the compliance dashboard (registration progress, completed checklist items, outstanding tasks, reminders, guides and recently viewed resources); clearly mark it as demo data, not verified personal business records.

## [x] 2. Business Registration Centre
- Explain business registration, common structures (sole proprietorship, partnership and limited company), common requirements and documents, and beginner-friendly registration steps.
- Link clearly to official BRS and eCitizen resources; include common FAQs. Do not fabricate procedures, fees or legal requirements.

## [x] 3. Permits and Licences Centre
- Explain permits, why licences may be needed, common permit categories, county business permits, sector-specific licences, renewal and compliance.
- Let users explore educational guidance by business type and location; describe how to identify potentially relevant permits and confirm specifics with the relevant authority. Do not state unverified requirements as fact.

## [x] 4. Tax Compliance Information Centre
- Explain business taxation, tax registration, KRA PIN, VAT, PAYE, filing/payment timing at a high level, record keeping and practical compliance habits using plain language.
- Link to official Kenya Revenue Authority resources. Do not invent tax rates, deadlines, procedures or legal requirements.

## [x] 5. Interactive Registration Checklist
- Provide the checklist steps: choose a business name; select a structure; identify registration requirements; prepare necessary documents; complete registration; obtain registration documents; identify applicable permits; understand tax registration requirements; review ongoing compliance obligations.
- Provide interactive accessible checkboxes, a progress bar, percentage, completion message, browser LocalStorage progress saving and a reset option.
- Do not store confidential legal or financial documents in LocalStorage.

## [x] 6. Regulatory Requirements Explorer
- Allow users to select business type, location, sector and business size, then display relevant general educational guidance, potential permits, registration information and compliance considerations.
- Clearly distinguish general guidance from requirements that need confirmation with the relevant authority.

## [x] 7. Guides and Support
- Include regulatory guides, FAQs, common registration challenges, official resources and relevant government-agency contact/resource information.
- Include an accessible support request form with labels and input validation; do not collect unnecessary sensitive information.
- Clearly state that AccessBridge does not provide official legal representation or government approval.

## [x] 8. Technical, accessibility, security and source requirements
- Use separate `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js` and `README.md` files, with clean reusable and well-commented code using HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
- Make navigation, buttons, forms, filters, explorer and checklist functional. Use LocalStorage only for checklist progress in this prototype.
- Use semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly navigation and accessible form labels. Protect personal information, avoid unnecessary sensitive information, and identify external official links.
- Kenya focus; official sources include Business Registration Service https://brs.go.ke/, Kenya Revenue Authority https://www.kra.go.ke/, eCitizen https://www.ecitizen.go.ke/, Kenya Law https://new.kenyalaw.org/, and Kenya National Chamber of Commerce and Industry https://kenyachamber.or.ke/.
- Do not fabricate legal provisions, government procedures, fees, tax rates, deadlines or licensing requirements.
