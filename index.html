01 BUSINESS REGISTRATION CENTRE

Build on a solid foundation.

Business registration is how you formally record your business with the right authority. The best structure depends on your plans—there's no one-size-fits-all answer.

Visit BRS (opens in a new tab)
ONE OWNER

Sole proprietorship

A business owned by one person. A business name and a separate company are not the same thing—check what fits your situation.

Explore BRS guidance (opens in a new tab)
SHARED OWNERSHIP

Partnership

Two or more people running a business together. Agreeing on roles and decision-making early can help avoid confusion later.

Explore BRS guidance (opens in a new tab)
A REGISTERED COMPANY

Limited company

A separate legal entity registered through the Companies Registry. Governance and reporting can differ from simpler structures.

02 PERMITS & LICENCES

Find out what your business might need.

A permit or licence is an approval from an authority to carry out certain activities. What applies can depend on where you operate and what you do.

County & sector can matter
CHECK LOCALLY

County business permits

County processes and permit names can vary. Check with the county where your business operates for current steps, fees and renewals.

Explore Kenya's official procedure guide (opens in a new tab)
CHECK YOUR ACTIVITY

Sector-specific approvals

Examples to ask about—not a checklist of requirements—include county trade permits, food or public-health guidance for relevant activities, and specialist sector regulators.

Explore the Kenya Trade Portal (opens in a new tab)
STAY ON TOP OF IT

Renewals & changes

Keep a note of any expiry or renewal dates provided by the issuing authority. Re-check when your location or business activity changes.

04 TAX COMPLIANCE

Tax basics, without the jargon.

Your obligations depend on factors like legal structure, activity and circumstances. KRA is the source for the current rules that apply to you.

Visit KRA (opens in a new tab)
START HERE

KRA PIN

A PIN identifies you in tax matters and is used for some official transactions. Ask KRA which registration path and obligations fit your business.

KRA: apply for a PIN (opens in a new tab)
INDIRECT TAX

VAT

VAT is a tax connected to taxable supplies of goods and services. Registration and reporting rules can change—check KRA's latest VAT guidance.

KRA: VAT information (opens in a new tab)
IF YOU EMPLOY STAFF

PAYE

PAYE relates to tax withheld from employees' employment income. If you hire people, confirm employer obligations directly with KRA.

KRA: PAYE information (opens in a new tab)
EVERYDAY HABIT

Records & filing

Keep business income and expense records organised. Filing periods and payment dates depend on the tax obligation—follow KRA's current instructions and notices.

Explore KRA online services (opens in a new tab)

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things founders ask.

Still unsure? Always confirm your situation with the relevant authority.

Talk to us
Is AccessBridge an official government service?

No. AccessBridge is an independent educational prototype. It does not represent an agency, give legal representation or issue government approvals. Use linked official sources for current rules and decisions.

Do I need a permit before I can start?

It depends on what your business does and where it operates. Ask the relevant county and any sector regulator to confirm which approvals, if any, apply to your specific activity.

Can I use a business name like a registered company?

They are different registration concepts. Compare the current BRS information for each structure and choose based on your circumstances; consider independent professional advice if you're unsure.

Will this site tell me my exact tax obligations?

No. Tax obligations depend on your circumstances and may change. AccessBridge explains some common terms and points you to KRA so you can confirm current requirements.

Where do I go if I'm stuck during registration?

Start with the BRS or eCitizen help pages linked below. For a question about your specific application, contact the agency handling that service through its official website.
